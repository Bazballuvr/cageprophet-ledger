How the ledger works

  • Every explicit written call is logged. If a post names a winner, it goes on the ledger. Hedged or conditional posts, polls, and image-only picks are listed below as not graded, so nothing is hidden.
  • Misses stay. Nothing is deleted or rewritten after the fight.
  • A cancelled bout is no action. It doesn't count either way.
  • Results come from official sources. The sources are listed below, and each call links to its original post on X.
  • Winner and method are graded separately. The right fighter with the wrong method is a hit on the winner and a miss on the method.
